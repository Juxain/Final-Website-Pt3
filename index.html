We Are Here To Make You Feline Fine

About Us

Whisker Wellness Grooming is a grooming service specifically targeted to our adorable feline friends. Cats notice everything, so we keep our space calm and quiet with no rush and no barking dogs. Every visit is designed to be low-stress for both you and your pet.

From gentle baths and brush-outs to nail trims, ear cleaning, and coat care, we treat each cat in a dedicated and individual way. Before we begin, we take time to learn your cat's personality, coat type, and comfort level, then we work at their pace and give them breaks whenever they need one. We tailor each appointment to fit whatever breed your companion is.


Why Choose Us?

  • A calm, cats-only space: No barking dogs and no crowded waiting room, just a quiet environment that keeps your cat relaxed.

  • Gentle, patient handling: We work at your cat's pace, offer breaks when they need them, and never rush a nervous kitty.

  • Care that fits your cat: Every appointment is tailored to your cat's coat, age, and personality, from fluffy long-haired companions to first-time kittens.

  • Cat-friendly products: We use gentle shampoos and tools chosen to be comfortable on sensitive skin and fur.

  • Clear prices and easy booking: You know the cost before you arrive, and booking an appointment takes only a minute.
